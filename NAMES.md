# Onomasticon — Names for the Accelerated World

Brand, media, polity and institution names mined from the Lexicon's roots and the e/acc pantheon. A companion to *Lexicon Accelerationis*. It keeps growing.

> **Before using any name:** search the trademark registers (INPI, EUIPO, USPTO), company registries and domains. Known collisions are marked ⚠.
